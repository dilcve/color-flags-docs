Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Color Flags ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The App is intended for a general audience (13+) and is not directed at children under 13.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email, or other directly identifying details to play. The App and the third-party services it uses may collect:

DataPurposeService
App usage & in-game events (screens viewed, flags completed, settings)Analytics, to improve the gameGoogle Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel
Device & advertising identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID), approximate data for adsTo show ads and limit ad frequencyGoogle AdMob
Purchase status (e.g. "remove ads")To unlock the ad-free experienceGoogle Play Billing / Apple App Store
Game progress (stars, achievements) tied to your platform accountOptional cross-device syncGoogle Play Games / Apple iCloud
Crash & diagnostic dataStabilityGoogle Play / Apple

2. How information is used

To operate and improve the App, display and cap advertising, process purchases, optionally sync your progress across your own devices, and keep the App stable.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. Where required by law (e.g. the EU/EEA, UK), we present a consent choice before personalized ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

4. Cloud sync

If you enable it, your game progress (stars and achievements only) is stored in your own Google Play Games or Apple iCloud account so it can sync between your devices. We do not receive or store this in our own servers, and your purchase status is not synced this way. No login inside the App is required.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Third-party services

The third parties above process data under their own privacy policies: Google (Firebase, AdMob, Play), Mixpanel, Apple.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU, LGPD in Brazil, CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise them, contact us below.

8. Data retention

Analytics and advertising data are retained per the providers' policies. Cloud sync data lives in your platform account until you delete the App data or your saved game.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

10. Contact

Questions? Email colorflagsapp@gmail.com.
